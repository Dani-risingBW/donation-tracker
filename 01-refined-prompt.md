# Refined Prompt: 25:35 Fundraiser Web App

> **Build a web application for our fundraiser, "25:35", that tracks progress toward a dollar goal and an item-donation drive.**
>
> **Core features**
> 1. **Progress tracker:** a dynamic progress bar showing total raised vs. goal. Totals combine cash donations and the dollar value of donated goods.
> 2. **Donation entry:** a form where a user logs either money or physical items. Items convert to cash value automatically using a per-item value.
> 3. **Item checklist:** quantities collected vs. target for:
>    - Knit Beanies
>    - Warm Mittens
>    - Fleece Scarves
>    - Cold Sandwiches (deli meat/cheese or PB&J)
>    - Fruit Cups & Soft Granola Bars
>    - Bottled Water
>    - Travel Toothbrush & Toothpaste Kits
>    - Travel Deodorant
>    - Wet Wipes (packets/packs)
>    - Feminine Hygiene Items (pads/tampons)
>    - Drawstring / Cinch Bags
> 4. **Where to Donate page:** our Cash App $cashtag and QR code, plus drop-off/item instructions.
> 5. **Cash App verification flow:** after donating, a donor submits a form with the amount and a screenshot. Submissions go to a review queue where an admin verifies the donation before it counts toward the progress bar.
> 6. **Admin role:** a protected dashboard to approve/reject submissions, edit the tracker, set goals and item values, and add manual entries.
> 7. **Contact:** spreadmattew2535@gmail.com in the footer and on a Contact section.
>
> **Branding and content**
> - Name: **25:35**
> - Scripture under the title: *"For I was hungry and you gave me something to eat, I was thirsty and you gave me something to drink, I was a stranger and you invited me in."* (Matthew 25:35)
> - Mission statement: A college-student outreach non-profit organization in Washington, D.C., dedicated to joyfully serving, praying for, and giving to the unhoused community through faith in God.
> - Colors: gold/sunflower `#D99B26`, light cream `#F9F6F0` (primary), navy `#001F3F`.
> - Navigation: clean, text-style buttons (modeled on campusoutreachdc.org/give), collapsed into a dropdown menu.
>
> **Additional requirements:** mobile-first, compact layout, privacy protection for screenshots, spam prevention, public view showing only verified totals.
